# Accounts

Who posts the micro content and what each account may say. The kit reads the table and the settings; the writer agents read the sections below.

## Accounts

| id | name | role | voice |
|---|---|---|---|
| <id> | <Full name> | <one line: who they are and what they do, e.g. "founder; makes the videos and talks to customers"> | <path to their voice file, relative to the workspace, or company> |
| <company id> | <Company> | company page; speaks as the company ("we"), never in the first person singular | company |

- **id:** short, lower case, no spaces. It goes into folder names, so it never starts an idea slug.
- **role:** a free-text line. Matching reads it to decide who can tell each idea as their own. A role that starts with "company" marks the company page.
- **voice:** a file, or `company` for the company voice (the `company voice` key in `videopowers.md`). A missing file also means the company voice.
