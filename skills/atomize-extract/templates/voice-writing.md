## Post shape (LinkedIn)
- The hook is the first one to three lines, under 210 characters, before "see more". It carries a real tension: a number, a scene, an objection, a quote.
- Short paragraphs, one idea each.
- 120 to 260 words for a post with an image or carousel.
- One ask at the end: a question the reader can answer from their own work. Never "comment a keyword".
- Links never in the caption; the first comment holds the video link and the sources.
- Numbers as digits, so every number can be checked.
