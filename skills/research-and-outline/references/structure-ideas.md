# Structure ideas (lower priority)

Ideas from outside Hormozi for checking the structure of an outline or a
script. **They have not been checked against their sources yet.** They are
kept here so the skills can grow from them later.

How to use them:
- Only as a second look, after the hook, retain and reward checklists
  (`hook-retain-reward.md`) and the craft rules have passed.
- Never to override the outline, the speaker file or those checklists.
- When one helps, say so in one line in your reply. When one doesn't fit,
  skip it.

| Idea | Where it's from | What to check |
|---|---|---|
| **But / therefore** | Trey Parker and Matt Stone (South Park) | Do scenes, or chapters, join with "but" or "therefore", never "and then"? (Already craft rule 14 in script-writing.) |
| **And, But, Therefore** | Randy Olson, scientist and filmmaker | Can each chapter be said in one sentence: "We know X, **and** Y. **But** Z. **Therefore**…"? If not, the chapter may not have a point yet. |
| **Re-hooks** | YouTube creators, general practice | Does each new section win attention back, usually by opening its next question just before the last one closes? (Close to the mini hook and the hand-over.) |
| **Plan for the dip** | The MrBeast production memo | The memo treats the first minute, the next few minutes and a later "re-engagement" moment as separate jobs. Is there a fresh surprise, number or turn a few minutes in, where attention usually drops? |
| **Problem → agitate → solve** | Classic copywriting | Is the problem made to feel costly (agitate) before the fix arrives? This is where the hook's **stakes** come from. |
